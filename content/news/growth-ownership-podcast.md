Joshua Pritikin joined *The Growth & Ownership Podcast* for "Beyond Belief: Trauma, Spirituality, Psychedelics, and the Psychology of Human Potential," exploring the idea that genuine religious or spiritual experience may not depend on traditional belief. The conversation examines how psychology, trauma, dissociation, meditation, psychedelics, and spiritual exploration intersect, and why the sequence in which people approach these practices may matter.

The episode also offers a psychological perspective on the growing tendency to treat AI as a "someone" rather than simply a tool, including how Internal Family Systems can help explain that impulse, while inviting listeners to question their assumptions about belief, consciousness, spirituality, healing, and human potential.
