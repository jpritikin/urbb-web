Matt Bianca reviewed *Religion Unburdened by Belief* on his Substack, [The Gentle Law](https://thegentlelaw.substack.com/), calling it "an ambitious, sprawling, and genuinely provocative work" whose central claim, that minimizing belief opens pathways to religious experience, is "counterintuitive yet compelling."

Matt Bianca holds a PhD in Education and Socio-Educational Intervention (University of Oviedo), with academic training in Pali and Sanskrit, a Diploma in Advanced Buddhist Studies (University of Mumbai), and an MBSR certificate (Palouse Mindfulness Center). He teaches early Buddhist texts at The Gentle Law, a non-sectarian school for modern practitioners.
