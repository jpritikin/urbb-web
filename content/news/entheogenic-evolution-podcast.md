Joshua Pritikin joined Martin W. Ball, Ph.D., host of *The Entheogenic Evolution* since 2008, for a wide-ranging conversation about *Religion Unburdened by Belief: The Way of Open Inquiry*.

Having entered both Internal Family Systems and psychedelic experience relatively recently in life, Joshua discusses what inspired him to write a thick book asking big questions about human experience and the mystery of being. As a regular attendee at the Church of the Holy Light of the Queen, the Santo Daime church in Ashland, Oregon, he also digs into a unique way that religious experience can be facilitated by psychedelic use.
