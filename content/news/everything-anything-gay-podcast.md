Joshua Pritikin joined Zach on the *Everything & Anything...and a bit gay Podcast* for "Ep. 339: Religion Without Belief: Psychedelics, IFS & Altered States." The conversation covers how Internal Family Systems "parts" show up in everyday mixed feelings and help define altered states, a notepad trick that makes meditation feel effortless, and Santo Daime ceremonies, where group singing keeps participants present. They also discuss blending vs. unblending as the key to avoiding a bad trip, prohibition and harm reduction, content beliefs vs. process beliefs, and consciousness, near-death experiences, 5-MeO-DMT, and the afterlife.
