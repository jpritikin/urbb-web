Sam welcomed me to *The Conscious Consultant Hour* on Talk Radio NYC to discuss *Religion Unburdened by Belief*. We explored what remains when creeds, doctrines, and articles of faith are set aside, how to reclaim the word religion from its bureaucratic baggage, the role of meditation and psychedelics in direct encounter with mystery, and how to tell authentic religious experience from self-deception. We also got into the Way of Open Inquiry and Sacred Subtraction.
