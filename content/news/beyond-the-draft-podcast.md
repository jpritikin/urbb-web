Joshua Pritikin joined *Beyond the Draft* to unpack the paradox behind the title of *Religion Unburdened by Belief*: what if the less you believe, the closer you actually get to genuine religious experience? The conversation covers how trauma and dissociation show up inside both religious and psychedelic experience, and why sequencing matters as much as substance when it comes to psychedelic readiness.

He also brought a therapist's lens to the AI conversation.
