Joshua Pritikin joined the *Get Behind Me Jesus* podcast for "Father, Son, and Holy Therapist: Exploring Internal Family Systems and More," exploring the intersections of Internal Family Systems therapy, trinitarian theology, and identity. The conversation covers the distinction between beliefs and process beliefs, unblending vs. blending in IFS, religious frameworks as relational systems, and a relational approach to psychedelics as sacrament, including ego dissolution and connection to consensus reality as necessary parts of practicing presence with plant medicine.
