Joshua Pritikin joined host Dean Graves on *Infinitely One* to discuss *Religion Unburdened by Belief*, exploring the distinction between religious and spiritual experiences, the role of belief in religion and exploration, psychedelic experiences and their relation to spirituality, the neuroscience of consciousness and near-death experiences, Internal Family Systems and parts work for self-healing, free will and its impact on consciousness, and the path toward enlightenment.
