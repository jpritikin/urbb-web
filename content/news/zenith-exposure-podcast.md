Joshua joined the *Zenith Exposure* podcast for "Beyond Belief: Finding Freedom Through Curiosity," a conversation about whether spirituality can exist without rigid belief systems, and what happens when we approach our inner world with curiosity instead of judgment.

The episode covers what Internal Family Systems teaches about our inner world, how understanding our "parts" can lead to greater healing and self-compassion, exploring spirituality without being confined by belief, and why curiosity can be more powerful than certainty.
