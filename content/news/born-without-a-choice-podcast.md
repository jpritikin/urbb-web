Joshua joined the *Born Without a Choice* podcast for "Religion Unburdened: Psychology, Spirituality, Trauma & Finding Your Own Path," exploring what genuine spiritual experience can look like when it doesn't require holding tightly to every inherited belief.

The conversation covers how trauma and dissociation can influence spiritual experiences, why people can feel drawn toward spirituality without traditional religious certainty, and how different practices may serve different stages of personal growth.
