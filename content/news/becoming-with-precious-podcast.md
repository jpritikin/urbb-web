Joshua Pritikin joined Precious on *Becoming With Precious: The Journey of Personal Growth And Reinvention* to talk about Internal Family Systems offers a framework for rebuilding trust between the fragmented parts of the mind.

Key takeaways from the conversation:

- 🌱 True personal growth is about stripping away the burdens that no longer serve you, rather than constantly adding new habits.
- 💔 Trauma fractures your internal trust, causing different parts of your personality to adopt protective behaviors that hinder your progress.
- 🗣️ Internal Family Systems provides a framework to unpack mixed feelings and build a trusting dialogue between your conflicting inner voices.
- 🧘 Emotional regulation requires developing the skill to remain unblended from your triggered parts so you can respond thoughtfully instead of reacting impulsively.
